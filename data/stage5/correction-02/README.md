# Correction wave 2 proposal

This folder records the proposed and applied corrections to the 14 contradicted fields imported in Cite wave 2. `drafts.json` and `review.md` are the approval record. `approved-proposals.json` records the user's decisions, `fetched-pages.json` records the required page and quote checks, and `application.md` records the applied changes and independent judgments. The Cite wave 2 finder prompts, page text, judge prompts and responses remain in `../cite-02/judge/`.

The proposer inspected current official guidance and copied wording elsewhere in each affected node. Three separate judge sessions checked the new text after it was applied. Two Cite wave 2 contradictions, the LPA death notification and the England and Wales death registration scope, appear to reflect the judge reading a broader service page than the node describes; their proposed text clarifies the node's scope.
