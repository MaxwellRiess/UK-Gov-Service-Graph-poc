Child Disability Payment What Child Disability Payment is Last updated 21 Aug 2025 Contents What Child Disability Payment is Who should apply for Child Disability Payment What you need to apply How to apply for Child Disability Payment After you apply for Child Disability Payment Decision about your application Payments Extra support with costs Child Disability Payment helps with the extra costs a disabled child might have. You can apply for children under 16 with a physical or mental disability. You can also apply if the child does not have a diagnosis. You can tell Social Security Scotland about: how they're affected any symptoms they have Child Disability Payment is tax-free and made up of a care component and a mobility component. A child's care and mobility needs can include physical or non-physical difficulties such as: emotional behavioural mental developmental learning Care component There are 3 payment rates for the care component. Children 3 months old or older can get the care component. Lowest The weekly rate is £30.30. It's for a child who, because of their physical or mental disability, either: needs help from another person, for much of the day, to manage their bodily functions is 16 or older and cannot prepare a cooked meal Middle The weekly rate is £76.70. It's for a child who, because of their physical or mental disability, either: needs help from somebody throughout the day to support with their bodily functions needs help from somebody throughout the night to support with their bodily functions needs supervised throughout the day to avoid danger to themselves or others needs supervised throughout the night to avoid danger to themselves or others gets renal dialysis treatment, during the day or at night, at least twice a week Highest The weekly rate is £114.60. It's for a child, who because of their physical or mental disability, either: needs help from somebody throughout the day and night to support with their bodily functions needs supervised throughout the day and night to avoid danger to themselves or others gets renal dialysis treatment, during the day and at night, at least twice a week has a terminal illness If a child has a terminal illness they will get the care component from birth. Children getting the highest care component rate also get Child Winter Heating Payment. Mobility component There are 2 payment rates for the mobility component. Children 3 or older can qualify for the mobility component Lower This is a weekly rate of £30.30. It's for a child who: is 5 or older can walk, but needs help from another person to move around outdoors Higher This is a weekly rate of £80. It's for a child who is 3 or older and either: unable to walk unable to walk outdoors whose health would get worse from walking has no legs or feet has a severe visual disability is blind and deaf has a severe mental disability with severe behavioural difficulties has a terminal illness You can apply to lease an accessible vehicle if the child gets the higher rate mobility payment. Warning You may have got an email from Social Security Scotland about the annual increase to your Child Disability Payment. If you need more information, contact Social Security Scotland .
