Legal risks or ‘liability’ Sole trader businesses have ‘unlimited liability’ which means owners are personally responsible for all of the debts of the business. If something goes wrong, you will have less protection. You may be able to get more protection with business insurance . Finances You keep all the profits after paying tax.
